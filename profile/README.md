# CygnusX Labs

### AI for Science · Trustworthy Research Agents · Reproducible Discovery

**FROM STARDUST, TO DISCOVERY.**

CygnusX Labs is an open-source research and engineering initiative building **trustworthy AI for Science infrastructure**. We connect scientific questions with research agents, expert-governed workflows, controlled computing, and traceable evidence.

Our goal is not simply to make AI run more analyses. It is to help researchers **understand what was done, verify how it was done, and build on the results with confidence**.

> **From research questions to reproducible scientific evidence.**

---

## What We Believe

- **Science first.** AI assists scientific reasoning; researchers remain responsible for critical methodological and interpretive decisions.
- **Freedom to explore, discipline to deliver.** Exploratory Python/R analysis and governed, versioned workflows serve different purposes.
- **Methods matter.** Re-running code is not the same as validating the scientific method behind it.
- **Evidence should travel with results.** Data sources, parameters, environments, execution records, and artifacts should be connected wherever feasible.
- **Research infrastructure should compound.** Reviewed methods, reusable workflows, and lessons from real projects should become durable research assets.

## How We Approach Trustworthy Research

**Research question → Data discovery → Research plan → Governed computation → Artifacts & evidence → Scientific review**

We are working toward an architecture in which:

1. **Research Agents** interpret scientific goals, coordinate specialized capabilities, and propose analysis plans.
2. **FlowFrame and domain workflows** provide structured scientific methods, validation boundaries, and versioned computation.
3. **Execution governance** separates exploratory work from approved workflows through permissions, checks, and human review where necessary.
4. **Evidence and BioReport** connect outputs to their inputs, methods, execution context, and scientific interpretation.

**Reproducibility is a goal we test, not a label we assume.** Features and scientific workflows may be at different stages of development, review, or real-world validation.

## Where We Are Going

Our priorities include reliable end-to-end research workflows, stronger scientific method governance, transparent provenance, research-ready reporting, and reusable domain-specific extensions.

The long-term vision is a shared AI for Science foundation that can support **life sciences, agriculture, and other data-intensive research disciplines** without rebuilding the core infrastructure for every field.
